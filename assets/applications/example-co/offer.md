# Senior Platform Engineer — Developer Productivity

**Company:** Vantage Cloud Ltd
**Location:** Remote (EU)

## About the role
Vantage Cloud is hiring a Senior Platform Engineer to own CI/CD reliability and Kubernetes
cost efficiency for our product engineering organisation, and to help teams ship faster with
better observability into the platform they run on.

## Required
- 5+ years operating Kubernetes-based infrastructure in production
- A track record of cutting infrastructure spend or improving CI reliability, backed by numbers
- Experience owning CI/CD pipelines end-to-end (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, GitLab CI, or similar)
- Comfortable building observability/monitoring pipelines (Prometheus, ELK, or equivalent)

## Responsibilities
- Drive Kubernetes-fleet cost optimisation and monitoring rollouts across product teams
- Own CI reliability and lead release-engineering process improvements
- Define and track developer-productivity metrics such as lead time for changes
